## A. Homepage and General FAQs

### 1. What does CubiclePro provide?

CubiclePro provides commercial restroom cubicles, urinal modesty panels, Junior Cubicles, HPL lockers, shower cubicles, changing-room cubicles, Pro Doors, washroom cladding, washbasin storage and coordinated profile and hardware options.

### 2. Can CubiclePro systems be customised?

Yes. Dimensions, layout, privacy, compatible profiles, hardware and configuration can be reviewed for every applicable product, subject to site conditions, technical feasibility and the approved project specification.

### 3. Which panel materials are offered?

The public material categories are Compact HPL and BWP-FR High-Density Board. Final material grade, panel thickness, hardware and configuration are confirmed against the approved project specification.

### 4. Which panel thicknesses are available?

Typical panel thicknesses offered are 12 mm and 18 mm. Other suitable thicknesses are available depending on project requirement and approved specification. Product components such as locker doors, shelves or dividers may follow a separate approved schedule.

### 5. Does CubiclePro provide installation?

Installation can be included according to the agreed project scope, location and site readiness. The quotation should state whether the requirement is supply-only or supply and installation.

### 6. How can I request a quotation?

Use Request a Quote, call or WhatsApp the sales team on +91 84011 18340, or email sales@cubiclepro.in. Share the project city, application, approximate quantity and drawings if available.

### 7. Where should I send a technical question?

Use Technical Enquiry for drawings, compatibility, profiles, hardware, mounting and site-interface questions. Technical WhatsApp must open +91 99247 31671 rather than the sales number.

### 8. Can colours and finishes be selected online?

No. The website does not provide a shade selector. Colours and finishes are finalised project-wise against the approved specification and physical selection process.

### 9. Does CubiclePro show completed client projects on the website?

Only verified project material may be identified as a completed or client project. Other approved images are labelled Product Visual, System Visual or Application Visual.

### 10. What warranty does CubiclePro provide?

CubiclePro provides a one-year warranty on hardware, profiles and installation workmanship, subject to normal usage and CubiclePro warranty conditions. Panel warranty applies according to the approved material grade and the respective manufacturer's warranty terms.

## B. Restroom Cubicle Category FAQs

### 1. Which restroom cubicle systems are available?

The restroom range includes Titan Black, Nova, Supernova, Supernova+, Base Box, Base Box Pro, Float and Sky Hung. Pro Doors is presented as a separate major product for suitable existing or project-specific openings.

### 2. How do I compare cubicle systems?

Compare the application, profile or support family, hardware, mounting condition, panel specification, architectural character and verified site interfaces. A product image alone is not a complete specification.

### 3. Can restroom cubicle dimensions be customised?

Yes. Dimensions and configuration can be reviewed for the site, subject to clearances, technical feasibility and the approved drawing and specification.

### 4. Are the systems suitable for wet areas?

Suitability depends on the selected panel grade, hardware, detailing and actual exposure. The exact wet-area requirement must be confirmed against the approved project specification.

### 5. Are all cubicle systems made with the same profiles and hardware?

No. Each system has a defined profile or support and hardware configuration. Grades and components must not be transferred from one product to another.

### 6. What information is needed before selecting a system?

Provide the application, expected usage, room layout, cubicle count, wall/floor/ceiling conditions, privacy requirement, material preference and design direction.

### 7. Can CubiclePro help with accessible cubicle planning?

CubiclePro can coordinate the cubicle system with the approved accessible layout. Final clearances, manoeuvring space, door direction and compliance remain subject to the project's applicable requirements and design approval.

### 8. Are indicator locks and door knobs both provided?

The approved door hardware arrangement should show and specify both the occupancy indicator lock and the separate door knob where required by that system.

### 9. Where are hinges and coat hooks installed?

Hinges and coat hooks are internal components. They should not be shown incorrectly on the public face of the cubicle door.

### 10. What is confirmed before production?

Final material grade, thickness, dimensions, door direction, profiles, hardware, support arrangement, finish and installation scope are confirmed through the approved project documents.

## D. Nova FAQs

Nova uses anodised aluminium profiles with SS 316 hardware.

## E. Supernova FAQs

### 1. What material grades are used in Supernova?

Supernova uses SS 304 profiles or supports with SS 316 hardware.

### 2. Is all Supernova metal SS 316?

No. Its profile or support grade is SS 304, while its hardware grade is SS 316.

### 3. Can Supernova dimensions be customised?

Yes. Dimensions and configuration can be reviewed for the approved layout and site conditions.

Nova uses anodised aluminium profiles. Supernova uses SS 304 profiles or supports. Both use SS 316 hardware.

### 5. When should Supernova be considered?

Consider it where a stainless-steel system expression is preferred and its profile, hardware and mounting configuration suit the project.

## R. HPL Locker FAQs

### 1. Which locker configurations are available?

The approved range includes Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, Tier 4, Tier 5 and Z-Type, with custom configurations reviewed separately.

### 2. What do the tier numbers mean?

Tier 1 means 1 door per column, Tier 2 means 2 doors per column, and the same pattern continues through Tier 5.

### 3. What is a Z-Type locker?

Z-Type is an interlocking module designed to balance hanging length with compact storage.

### 4. Are locker dimensions standard?

No universal size should be presented. Lockers are custom-built to site dimensions and client requirements, with final internal configuration confirmed against the approved locker schedule.

### 5. Do CubiclePro lockers use legs?

Do not describe the locker base as “legs.” The base and support condition must follow the approved locker construction description.

### 6. What panel thicknesses are used in the approved locker construction?

The approved master content records 9 mm Compact HPL for doors and top/bottom/base components, and 3 mm Compact HPL for side and back components. The final schedule must still be checked for the selected locker module.

### 7. What accessories are included?

The approved construction includes aluminium and stainless-steel accessories, SS hinges, an aluminium interlocking support frame and individual locksets as specified. Optional hanger rods are included only where the exact module record states them.

### 8. Can locker banks be customised?

Yes. Module mix, bank dimensions, internal arrangement and locking can be reviewed against user needs and site conditions.

### 9. How are Tier 6 and Z-Type Tier 2 handled?

They are currently treated as custom configurations until complete approved product information is available. The supplied locker illustration remains unchanged.

### 10. Should a locker card open directly to the enquiry form?

No. It should first open the relevant product-detail page with use, configuration and technical information, followed by an enquiry CTA.

## V. Accessories, Profiles and Hardware FAQs

### 1. Which accessory groups are available?

The range is organised into Profiles and Hardware. Profiles include black powder-coated aluminium, anodised aluminium and stainless-steel families; hardware follows the approved product configuration.

### 2. Can accessories be selected independently of the cubicle system?

Compatibility must be checked. Profiles, locks, hinges, knobs, legs, channels and rails should be coordinated with the selected system and panel.

## W. Application FAQs

### Corporate offices

**What should an office washroom specification prioritise?**  
User flow, visitor and staff use, privacy, fit-out interfaces, cleaning access, maintainable hardware and the intended interior language.

**Can office cubicles and lockers be coordinated together?**  
Yes. The restroom, staff changing and storage scope can be coordinated while keeping each product's schedule clear.

### Education

**How should a school select cubicles?**  
Begin with age group, supervision, privacy, hardware reach, accessible provision and the approved LittleSteps, Explorer, Horizon or Youth record.

**Can schools use a mixture of adult and Junior Cubicles?**  
Yes, where the approved room plan calls for age-specific, staff or accessible facilities. Each configuration must remain clearly identified.

### Healthcare

**What is important for healthcare washrooms?**  
Access, assisted use, cleaning workflow, maintenance, service interfaces and documented performance for the exact selected material grade.

**Are hygiene claims automatic?**  
No. Publish antibacterial or specialist hygiene claims only when the selected grade has supporting documentation.

### Hospitality

**Can hospitality washrooms combine cubicles, showers and storage?**  
Yes. Coordinate the dry and wet zones, privacy, cladding, washbasin area, lockers and maintenance access as one project scope.

**How are colours selected?**  
Colours and finishes are finalised project-wise; the website does not provide an online shade selector.

### Airports and transit

**What should high-use public facilities confirm?**  
Peak usage, circulation, accessible facilities, maintainable components, cleaning access, installation phasing and documented panel requirements.

**Does a clear-floor system always suit an airport?**  
No. Float or Sky Hung should be considered only after the wall or ceiling support conditions are verified.

### Industrial facilities

**Which products may be relevant to industrial sites?**  
Restroom cubicles, changing-room cubicles, shower cubicles, UMPs and locker banks may be coordinated around shifts and site conditions.

**What information helps with industrial locker planning?**  
Share shift size, uniforms or equipment stored, changing flow, room dimensions, locking method and required compartment count.

### Retail and public spaces

**What matters in customer-facing public washrooms?**  
User mix, clear circulation, privacy, accessible facilities, cleaning access and a system that suits the interior and maintenance plan.

**Can custom dimensions be used?**  
Yes, subject to site verification, technical feasibility and approved project documents.

### Sports and wellness

**How should sports facilities plan washrooms and changing areas?**  
Coordinate wet and dry zones, showers, changing privacy, benches, lockers, peak use, drainage and cleaning access.

**Which locker type is best for a gym?**  
The correct type depends on stored items, user count, internal arrangement, aisle space and locking requirement rather than one universal tier.
